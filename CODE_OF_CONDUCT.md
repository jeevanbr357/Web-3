# Code of Conduct

## Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

### Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone—regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

### Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Showing empathy toward others

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that is sexualized in nature or promotes hate

---

### Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Clarifying acceptable behavior
- Responding to issues fairly and consistently
- Taking corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior
- Maintaining the integrity of the project environment

---

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within:
- All project spaces (GitHub repos, issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Public platforms where an individual represents the project
- Online or offline community events related to the project
